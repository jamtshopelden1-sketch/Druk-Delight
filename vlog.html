Ema Datshi: The National Dish of Bhutan

Ema Datshi is the heart of Bhutanese cuisine. Made with chilies and cheese, this dish is spicy, creamy, and deeply satisfying. It is served with red rice and enjoyed daily by many Bhutanese families.

Bhutanese Food Mar 16, 2026
Momos: Bhutan’s Favorite Snack

Momos are soft dumplings filled with meat or cheese. They are steamed or fried and served with spicy sauce, making them a popular street food and family favorite.

Sikam Paa: Dried Pork with Chilies

Sikam Paa is made from sun-dried pork cooked with chilies. It has a smoky flavor and crispy texture, making it a unique and delicious dish.

Bhutanese Food Mar 18, 2026
Red Rice: A Healthy Staple

Bhutanese red rice is nutritious and slightly nutty in flavor. It is served with almost every meal and pairs perfectly with spicy dishes

Bhutanese Food Mar 18, 2026
Kewa Datshi: Potato and Cheese Delight

Kewa Datshi is a comforting dish made with potatoes, chilies, and cheese. It is less spicy than Ema Datshi and loved for its smooth and rich flavor.
